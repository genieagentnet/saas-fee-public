Nestled at 1800m in the Saas Valley of the Valais canton, Saas-Fee is one of Switzerland's most extraordinary mountain villages. The village sits in a natural amphitheatre formed by 13 four-thousander peaks — mountains exceeding 4000m — including the Dom, the highest peak entirely within Switzerland. Above the rooftops, the Fee Glacier spills dramatically down from the heights of the Mittelallalin, giving the village its nickname: the "Pearl of the Alps." Here, traditional Walliser wooden chalets stand alongside modern mountain infrastructure, and the absence of cars means the village has an unhurried, timeless quality that's increasingly rare in the Alps.

## What Makes Saas-Fee Special

Saas-Fee stands apart from other Alpine resorts in several important ways:

- **Car-free village** — no private vehicles are permitted inside the village, creating a safe, peaceful atmosphere for walkers and families
- **Year-round glacier skiing** — the Mittelallalin glacier at 3600m keeps runs open from summer through to the start of the winter season, meaning you can ski in July and August
- **UNESCO Biosphere Reserve** — Saas-Fee and the surrounding Saas Valley are protected as a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, ensuring the preservation of the region's exceptional natural environment
- **Traditional Walliser architecture** — centuries-old timber granaries (mazots), raised on mushroom-shaped stadel stones to deter rodents, are scattered throughout the village alongside ornate wooden chalets
- **One of Switzerland's most scenic destinations** — the 360° panorama of four-thousanders is widely regarded as one of the finest mountain views in the entire Alps

## Seasons

Saas-Fee rewards visitors in every season, and the glacier means that the mountains are almost never truly "closed."

**Winter (December–April)** is peak season, with the full ski area open across more than 100km of pistes. Snowfall is reliable thanks to the altitude, and the village takes on a festive, buzzing energy — especially around Christmas and New Year. This is when Saas-Fee is at its liveliest, with ski schools, après-ski bars, and mountain restaurants all in full swing.

**Summer (July–September)** transforms Saas-Fee into a hiking paradise. Dozens of marked trails fan out from the village into alpine meadows, past glacial lakes, and up to high-altitude viewpoints. The glacier ski area remains open through the summer months, making Saas-Fee a favourite warm-weather training destination for professional ski teams. Temperatures in the village are comfortably warm, while the high peaks stay refreshingly cool.

**Spring and Autumn** are quieter shoulder seasons that appeal to visitors looking for lower prices, fewer crowds, and a more local atmosphere. Spring skiing in March and April offers long sunny days and soft snow. The golden larch forests of October are a highlight for walkers and photographers.

## Getting Around

Saas-Fee's car-free policy is one of its most loved features. Once you've left your vehicle at the Parkhaus at the valley entrance, the village is yours to explore entirely on foot. For longer distances or heavy luggage, a fleet of **electric taxis** operates throughout the village and is available on request. **Electric buses (e-buses)** connect key points around the village perimeter, and the extensive **mountain transport network** — gondolas, cable cars, and underground funicular — links the village to the slopes above.

Getting around is easy, enjoyable, and emission-free.

<Tip>
  If you're planning multiple days of exploring the mountain transport network, look into the **Saastal Card** — it provides unlimited use of cable cars, gondolas, and buses throughout the Saas Valley for the duration of your stay, and is often included with hotel bookings. Ask your accommodation provider when you book.
</Tip>
