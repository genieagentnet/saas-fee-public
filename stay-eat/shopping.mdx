Shopping in Saas-Fee's car-free village is a relaxed experience. The pedestrian streets are lined with ski and outdoor shops, souvenir boutiques, local food stores, and artisan workshops. Whether you need to gear up for the slopes or pick up gifts to take home, the village has you covered.

## Sports & Ski Equipment

Saas-Fee has a solid selection of ski and outdoor equipment shops serving both beginners and experienced mountain enthusiasts. Whether you need to hire kit for the week or invest in new gear for the season, you will find what you need in the village.

Rental equipment — skis, snowboards, boots, helmets, and poles — is available from multiple shops at the mountain base and throughout the village. One particularly convenient feature is the ability to rent your equipment from one location and return it to a different outlet, giving you flexibility if your plans change during the week.

Major international brands such as **Intersport** are represented, alongside local specialist shops staffed by knowledgeable locals who can advise on conditions and kit. These independent specialists are especially worth visiting if you want personalised advice on boot fitting, ski selection, or equipment tuning. In summer, the same shops typically shift their focus to hiking, mountaineering, mountain biking, and via ferrata equipment.

## Local & Swiss Products

Some of the best things to bring home from Saas-Fee are the edible and drinkable kind. The Valais canton produces outstanding food and drink — much of it difficult to find outside Switzerland.

- **Valais wine** — the Valais is one of Switzerland's most respected wine regions. Look for indigenous red grape varieties such as **Cornalin** and **Humagne Rouge**, which produce rich, characterful wines you rarely encounter elsewhere. For whites, **Fendant** (Chasselas) is the local classic — crisp, mineral, and perfect with fondue or raclette. A bottle or two makes an excellent gift.
- **Local cheese** — the Saas Valley and surrounding region produce excellent alpine cheeses. Pick up aged hard cheeses from the valley dairy for a taste of genuine mountain terroir.
- **Swiss chocolate** — Switzerland's chocolate reputation is well earned. You will find well-known names such as **Läderach** and **Lindt**, as well as smaller local chocolatiers selling handmade pralines and seasonal specialities. Stock up for the journey home.
- **Walliser Trockenfleisch** — air-dried meat from the Valais, produced using traditional methods and the dry Alpine air. The beef version is particularly prized: deep red, intensely flavoured, and paper-thin. It travels well and makes a memorable gift for food lovers.

## Souvenirs

If you want to take a piece of Switzerland home with you, Saas-Fee's souvenir shops have the classics covered alongside some more distinctive local options.

- **Cowbells** — the quintessential Swiss keepsake, ranging from small decorative versions to full-sized working bells
- **Carved wooden decorations** — hand-carved figures, bowls, and ornaments in the traditional alpine style
- **Embroidered textiles** — table runners, cushion covers, and decorative pieces featuring traditional Swiss mountain motifs
- **Swiss watches** — from affordable fashion watches to Swiss-made timepieces; a lasting reminder of your trip
- **Cuckoo clocks** — a classic choice, available in sizes to suit every mantelpiece and luggage allowance
- **Local alpine herbs and teas** — herbal blends made from plants grown in the high alpine meadows, often with packaging that captures the mountain landscape beautifully
- **Saas-Fee branded merchandise** — hats, fleeces, bags, and other items bearing the Saas-Fee name or glacier imagery, ideal as practical mementos of your trip

## Opening Hours

Most shops in Saas-Fee follow a traditional Swiss retail pattern, with a midday break and limited Sunday trading.

| Period | Hours |
|--------|-------|
| Monday – Saturday (standard) | 08:30 – 12:00 and 14:00 – 18:30 |
| Sunday | Limited opening; many shops closed |
| Peak ski season | Extended hours; many shops open through lunch and later into the evening |
| Between seasons (May, November) | Some shops may close entirely; check ahead if visiting during shoulder season |

## Supermarkets & Essentials

For everyday supplies, self-catering ingredients, and household essentials, the village has you covered.

- **Migros** — Switzerland's largest supermarket chain has a branch in the village. You will find a full range of groceries, fresh produce, ready meals, wine and beer, snacks, and household items. The Migros also stocks a reasonable selection of Valais wines at competitive prices.
- **Volg** — a smaller convenience store format common in Swiss mountain villages. Useful for top-up shops, fresh bread, dairy products, and essentials when you do not need a full supermarket run.
- **Apotheke (Pharmacy)** — the village pharmacy stocks prescription and over-the-counter medicines, sun protection (essential at altitude), blister plasters, and personal care items. Staff typically speak English and can advise on minor ailments.
