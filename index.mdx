Welcome to Saas-Fee — a magical, car-free Alpine village perched at 1800m in the heart of the Swiss Alps. Encircled by 13 four-thousander peaks and draped beneath the sweeping Fee Glacier, Saas-Fee is home to one of Europe's largest glacier ski areas and some of the continent's most spectacular summer hiking. Recognised as a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, the village has preserved its traditional Walliser character while offering world-class mountain experiences every season of the year. Whether you're arriving for powder runs in January, wildflower trails in July, or simply the peace of an Alpine village with no cars in sight, this guide covers everything you need.

<Note>
  Saas-Fee is a fully car-free village. All private vehicles must be left at the multi-storey car park (Parkhaus Saas-Fee) at the valley entrance before you enter. From there, you can walk to most accommodation, or arrange an electric taxi for luggage and transfers. Electric buses and gondolas connect the village to the slopes and surrounding area.
</Note>
