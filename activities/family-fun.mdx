Saas-Fee's car-free village makes it exceptionally safe and relaxed for families. Children can roam freely while parents enjoy the mountains without the constant worry of passing traffic. From dedicated ski kindergartens and glacier funicular rides to night sledging and Alpine flower walks, there is something engaging for every age group across both the winter and summer seasons.

## Winter Family Activities

Winter in Saas-Fee is magical for families, and the resort has invested heavily in making the mountain accessible and enjoyable from the very youngest ages.

**Ski kindergarten from age 3**
The Swiss Ski School (Skischule Saas-Fee) operates a dedicated ski kindergarten for children from three years old. Qualified instructors trained specifically in teaching young children run half-day and full-day programmes that combine skiing with games, songs, and snow play — making the learning experience genuinely enjoyable rather than intimidating. Children progress at their own pace, and parents can check in throughout the day.

**Designated beginner slopes**
The Mamamison and Spielboden areas are set aside for learners and younger skiers, away from faster intermediate and advanced traffic. Wide, gentle pistes and slow-moving beginner lifts give children the space and confidence to find their feet without feeling overwhelmed.

**Night sledging — the longest natural toboggan run in the Alps**
The Saas-Fee night sledge run stretches an extraordinary **5km** from Hannigalp down to the village — the longest natural toboggan run in the Alps. The run is illuminated on selected evenings throughout the winter season, and sleds can be hired in the village. This is consistently rated by families as one of the most memorable experiences in the resort.

**Snow-shoeing for the whole family**
Snowshoe trails around the village and lower slopes are well-marked and suitable for older children and adults. Equipment hire is available, and guided snowshoe walks with a local guide are a wonderful way to explore the quieter corners of the winter landscape.

**Ice skating rink**
The village ice rink is a popular afternoon activity, particularly on days when mountain weather keeps you off the higher slopes. Skate hire is available on-site. The rink hosts skating sessions for all ages and occasional evening events throughout the winter season.

## Summer Family Activities

Summer transforms Saas-Fee into a playground of a different kind, with a range of activities specifically suited to families travelling with children of all ages.

**Fee Glacier educational trail**
The Gletscherweg glacier trail at 2500m is one of the most family-friendly routes in the valley. Interpretation boards along the path explain how glaciers form, move, and change over time — turning the walk into an engaging learning experience that children and adults find equally fascinating. Accessible by cable car from the village.

**Indoor Fun Park**
On days when mountain weather closes in, the village **Indoor Fun Park** provides a welcome refuge. The facility includes climbing walls, trampolines, and a range of play equipment suitable for a variety of ages. A reliable plan B for wet afternoons.

**Family bike rides on the valley cycle path**
The Saas Valley cycle path runs along the valley floor on smooth, mostly flat terrain, making it ideal for family cycling outings with younger children. Bike hire — including children's bikes and tag-along attachments — is available from rental shops in the village.

**Mini golf and outdoor play areas**
The village mini golf course is a perennial favourite with families, and several outdoor play areas are dotted around the village for younger children. These are well-maintained and freely accessible throughout the summer season.

**Alpine flower walks with a guide**
The meadows above Saas-Fee are home to an extraordinary range of Alpine wildflowers, and guided botanical walks are available for families who want to learn about the plants that thrive in this high-altitude environment. Walks are led by local naturalist guides and pitched to be engaging for children as well as botanically curious adults.

## Mittelallalin Glacier Experience

For many families, the trip up to the **Mittelallalin glacier at 3456m** is the single most memorable experience of their Saas-Fee visit — and rightly so.

The journey itself is part of the adventure. You take the **Alpin Express gondola** from the village to Felskinn, then board the **Metro Alpin** — an underground funicular railway that runs entirely inside the mountain — to emerge at the glacier station at 3456m. The Metro Alpin is the highest underground railway in the world, and children find the experience thrillingly dramatic.

At the top, the **ice pavilion** is carved directly into the glacier ice and houses a permanent exhibition of ice sculptures. Walking through chambers sculpted from ancient glacier ice — lit to show its extraordinary blue-green colour — is an unforgettable experience for children and adults alike.

The **revolving restaurant** at the summit rotates slowly to offer a full 360-degree panorama of the surrounding Alps. A warm meal at the top of the world, with glaciers stretching in every direction, makes a fitting conclusion to the excursion before the journey back down to the village.

<Note>
  The village **electric taxis** can carry pushchairs, buggies, and luggage between accommodation and the lift stations — ask your hotel or apartment host to arrange one on arrival. Young children's ski equipment, including tiny boots and short skis, can be rented from the village hire shops at significantly reduced rates compared to adult equipment.
</Note>

## Kids' Passes

Saas-Fee is notably generous with its family lift pass pricing. **Children under 9 ski free** when accompanied by a paying adult, making the glacier experience genuinely accessible for families with young children.

**Youth passes** for ages 9–19 are available at discounted rates compared to adult prices. Combined family passes for two adults and children are also offered — check the current season's pricing on the official Saas-Fee website or at the lift ticket offices in the village, as rates and age thresholds are reviewed each season.

<Tip>
  Book ski school places **well in advance** for the Christmas and New Year period and the February school holidays. The Swiss Ski School fills its children's group lesson programmes weeks before these peak dates, and waiting until you arrive in the resort risks missing out entirely. Online pre-booking is available through the ski school website.
</Tip>
