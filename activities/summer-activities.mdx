Saas-Fee's summer season (June–September) is packed with outdoor adventure. The glacier keeps skiing on the calendar, but the warm months also bring mountain biking, climbing, paragliding, and a festival scene that rivals the winter buzz. Whether you are here for adrenaline or relaxation, the Saas Valley has a summer activity to match your pace.

## Mountain Biking

With more than **80km of dedicated bike trails**, Saas-Fee and the wider Saas Valley deliver a compelling destination for two-wheeled adventure. The network spans everything from smooth, wide valley paths ideal for a leisurely family ride to steep, rocky singletracks that will challenge even experienced riders.

Bike rental is available at several shops in the village, stocking a range of hardtails, full-suspension trail bikes, and — increasingly popular — **electric mountain bikes (E-MTBs)**. E-bike rental has transformed summer access to the higher trails for riders who want to explore further without the lung-busting climbs that traditional mountain biking demands at altitude. If you have not tried an E-MTB yet, Saas-Fee is an excellent place to start.

Several cable cars allow **bike transport**, meaning you can ride lifts to the high trailheads and enjoy long, flowing descents back to the village. Check the current season's lift schedule for bike-friendly opening times, as not all gondolas carry bikes at all hours.

## Climbing & Via Ferrata

The ring of four-thousanders surrounding Saas-Fee makes this one of the most sought-after climbing destinations in the Alps. Routes range from classic Alpine ridge climbs accessible to fit, experienced mountaineers to **via ferrata** — secured rock routes fitted with iron rungs, cables, and ladders — that open up dramatic vertical terrain to those without formal climbing experience.

Several via ferrata routes operate around the valley at varying grades of difficulty. Beginners can tackle the lower-grade routes with a hired harness and helmet from the village; more experienced climbers should explore the harder routes with a qualified mountain guide who knows the local rock.

**Guided climbing tours** are available through the Saas-Fee mountain guide association. Options include introductory half-day rock climbing courses, guided ascents of classic Alpine peaks such as the Allalinhorn (4027m) — the most accessible four-thousander in the area — and multi-day summit itineraries for serious alpinists.

## Paragliding & Aerial Activities

There are few ways to appreciate the scale of the Saas-Fee glacier and its surrounding peaks more viscerally than from the air. **Tandem paragliding flights** launch from Hannigalp at 2336m, giving you a bird's-eye view of the village, the glacier, and the valley below.

No experience is required for a tandem flight — you fly in a two-person harness with a qualified pilot who handles all aspects of the launch, flight, and landing. Flights typically last 15–30 minutes depending on thermal conditions. The views across the glacier and towards the Mischabel range are extraordinary.

Book your flight through local operators in the village — several companies offer tandem flights throughout the summer season. Morning flights tend to offer the smoothest conditions; afternoon thermals can produce a more dynamic (and exciting) experience for those who want it.

## Wellness & Spas

After days of hiking, biking, or climbing at altitude, your body will thank you for a recovery session in one of Saas-Fee's excellent wellness facilities. Several hotels open their **spa facilities to day visitors**, allowing non-guests to access pools, saunas, steam rooms, and treatment menus.

The **Aqua Allalin** pool and wellness centre is the resort's most popular public facility, combining indoor and outdoor swimming with a range of spa treatments. The combination of mountain air, warm water, and glacier views from the pool terrace is hard to beat.

For a more traditional Alpine wellness experience, **thermal baths** in the wider Saas Valley offer a deeply relaxing soak in mineral-rich water. These are particularly enjoyable on cooler summer evenings when the mountains are lit by the last of the day's sun.

## Golf

The **Saas-Fee Golf Club** operates a high-altitude course set against one of the most dramatic backdrops in Swiss golf. Open from June to October (snow conditions permitting), the course offers a genuinely unique Alpine golfing experience with stunning views of the surrounding peaks and glacier on every hole.

Green fees and club hire are available directly through the club. The course is popular in July and August, so book tee times in advance during peak season.

## Running & Trail Running

Saas-Fee has carved out a serious reputation on the international **trail running** calendar. The annual race calendar includes events that attract elite runners from across Europe alongside recreational participants, with routes taking in some of the most spectacular Alpine terrain accessible from the resort.

The **SwissAlpine** and related summer race events take place in July and August, drawing international fields and a festival atmosphere to the village on race weekends. Even if you are not racing, the routes used by the events are open to recreational runners throughout the season — the trail network gives you everything from fast, flat valley runs to lung-burning ascents of the high ridges.

All routes are well-marked and accessible without a guide, though runners heading into the higher zones should carry the same essentials — water, layers, and sun protection — as hikers venturing to altitude.
