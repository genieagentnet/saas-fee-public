Saas-Fee is one of Europe's few genuinely car-free mountain villages. Once you've parked at the Parkhaus (car park) at the village entrance, you navigate by foot, electric taxi, gondola, or mountain transport. This makes the village safe, peaceful, and exceptionally clean — and it means lifts and cable cars double as everyday transport, carrying you from your hotel door to high-altitude glacier terrain with ease.

## Village Transport

Getting around the village itself is refreshingly simple. Without cars to contend with, the streets belong to pedestrians and a small fleet of quiet electric vehicles.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="On Foot" icon="person-walking">
    The village centre is compact and walkable. Most accommodation, restaurants, and shops are within 10–15 minutes' walk of one another. Paths are well maintained and clearly signed throughout the season.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Electric Taxis" icon="bolt">
    Electric taxis operate from the car park and village centre. Book through your hotel reception or join the queue at the taxi rank. Taxis carry luggage and multiple passengers — ideal on arrival or when you're carrying equipment. A fare applies for all journeys.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Free Shuttle Buses" icon="bus">
    Free electric shuttle buses run on fixed routes within the village during peak season, connecting the car park, accommodation zones, and the main lift stations. Check the current timetable on arrival, as schedules vary by season.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Mountain Transport Network

Saas-Fee's lift network is one of the most impressive in the Alps, reaching from the village at 1800m all the way to the Mittelallalin glacier at 3456m. The main installations form a logical chain up the mountain, and each serves both skiers and summer visitors.

| Installation | Type | From | To | Altitude |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Alpin Express** | Gondola | Village (Saas-Fee) | Felskinn | 1800m → 2991m |
| **Metro Alpin** | Underground funicular | Felskinn | Mittelallalin | 2991m → 3456m |
| **Felskinn Cable Car** | Cable car | Felskinn | Glacier area | Upper glacier terrain |
| **Morenia Gondola** | Gondola | Mid-mountain | Mid-station | Mid-mountain access |
| **Hannigalp Gondola** | Gondola | Village | Hannigalp | Eastern slopes & panorama walks |
| **Langfluh Cable Car** | Cable car | Saas-Fee | Längfluh | Längfluh glacier area |

### Key Installations at a Glance

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Alpin Express" icon="cable-car">
    The primary route into the ski area. This large gondola departs from the village at 1800m and whisks you up to Felskinn at 2991m. It's the starting point for most ski runs and summer hikes, and sees the heaviest morning traffic — arrive early to beat the queues.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Metro Alpin" icon="train-subway">
    From Felskinn, the Metro Alpin underground funicular tunnels through the mountain to Mittelallalin at 3456m — making it the world's highest underground railway. It opens up the year-round glacier ski area and offers extraordinary panoramic views at the top station.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Hannigalp Gondola" icon="mountain">
    The Hannigalp gondola provides access to the resort's quieter eastern slopes and some of the best panorama walking trails in the area. It's a great alternative when the main ski area is busy, and offers spectacular views across the Saas Valley.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Langfluh Cable Car" icon="cable-car">
    The Langfluh cable car serves the Längfluh glacier area, a favourite among off-piste skiers and ski tourers. In summer, the Längfluh is a gateway to high-altitude glacier hiking routes and is accessible for non-skiers.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Operating Hours

Lifts in Saas-Fee generally operate from **08:30 to 16:30** during the main winter ski season, giving you a solid eight-hour window on the mountain. A few things to keep in mind:

- **Summer hours vary** by installation and month. The glacier lifts (Alpin Express, Metro Alpin) typically run through the summer season, but lower gondolas may operate on reduced schedules.
- **First and last lifts** can shift during school holidays and peak periods — check the Saas-Fee website for daily updates.
- **Closures for maintenance** occasionally affect individual lifts. The resort posts notices at the ticket office and online.
- **Night skiing and extended hours** are available on selected lifts during peak season. These sessions are popular — check the current schedule when you arrive.

Always check the [official Saas-Fee website](https://www.saas-fee.ch) for daily operating status, especially after heavy snowfall or strong winds.

## Accessibility

Saas-Fee's car-free environment and purpose-built mountain infrastructure make it one of the more accessible Alpine resorts in Europe.

- **Electric wheelchairs and mobility scooters** are permitted throughout the village and on the main paths.
- **Gondolas** — including the Alpin Express and Metro Alpin — can accommodate standard wheelchairs. Staff at each station are trained to assist.
- **Village paths** are kept clear of snow and ice during winter, and most routes between the main lift stations are navigable without steps.
- **Electric taxis** provide door-to-door transport for visitors who find walking distances challenging.

For specific accessibility needs — including adapted ski equipment hire, sit-ski lessons, or assistance at particular lift stations — contact the Saas-Fee Tourist Office before your arrival so arrangements can be made in advance.

<Note>
  Mountain transport is included with the Saastal ski pass and the Saastal Card (summer). You do not need to carry cash for the lifts if you have a valid pass — simply tap or scan your pass at each turnstile and go.
</Note>
