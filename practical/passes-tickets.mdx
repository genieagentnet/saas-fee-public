Choosing the right pass for your Saas-Fee visit is key to getting good value. Options range from single-day lift tickets to the multi-resort Magic Pass and the all-inclusive Saastal Card for summer visitors. Children's and youth discounts make family ski holidays more affordable, and buying online in advance nearly always saves you money over the ticket window price.

## Winter Ski Passes

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Day Pass">
    A day pass gives you unlimited access to all lifts and pistes in the Saas-Fee ski area for a single day. This is the best option if you're visiting for a short time or want to try Saas-Fee before committing to a multi-day pass.

    - **Typical adult price:** CHF 65–75 per day (prices vary by season and date)
    - **Half-day tickets:** available for late starters — covers lifts from midday onwards
    - **Afternoon tickets:** a budget-friendly option if you're arriving late or want a short session on the slopes
    - **Purchase options:** online at saas-fee.ch or at the main ticket office by the Alpin Express gondola

    Day passes are non-transferable and valid only for the date purchased. Online booking is recommended during peak periods when queues at the ticket office can be long.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Multi-Day Pass">
    Multi-day passes offer significant savings over purchasing daily tickets and are the best choice for most holiday visitors. Passes are loaded onto a rechargeable card that you scan at each lift turnstile.

    - **Available durations:** 3-day, 6-day, and 7-day passes
    - **Savings:** the longer the pass, the lower your effective daily rate — a 6-day pass can save you 15–20% compared to six individual day tickets
    - **Validity:** consecutive days only; the clock starts on your first day of use
    - **Flexibility:** if lifts are closed due to weather on a full closure day, resort policy may allow an extension — check current terms when purchasing

    Multi-day passes are the most popular choice for chalet and hotel guests staying a full week. If you're unsure whether you'll ski every day, a 6-day pass for a 7-day trip is often the smartest buy.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Season Pass">
    The Saastal season pass covers the entire Saas-Fee ski area for the full winter season — from the autumn glacier opening right through to spring closing. It's the ultimate commitment for those who'll be on the mountain regularly.

    - **Best for:** local residents, chalet owners, and visitors who plan multiple trips to Saas-Fee in the same season
    - **Coverage:** all marked pistes and lifts in the Saas-Fee ski area, including the Metro Alpin and glacier area
    - **Ski bus:** free ski bus between Saas-Fee, Saas-Grund, Saas-Almagell, and Saas-Balen is included
    - **Photo required:** a passport-style photo is needed for the season pass card — you can upload this when purchasing online

    Season pass holders also benefit from early-season discounts on ski school and equipment rental at some partner shops.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Magic Pass">
    The Magic Pass is a flat-fee annual ski pass covering more than 100 ski resorts across Switzerland and Austria — and Saas-Fee is included. It's one of the best-value products in Alpine skiing if you ski at multiple resorts throughout the season.

    - **Annual fee:** approximately CHF 399 for adults (prices may vary; check the current season's rate at magic-pass.ch)
    - **Coverage:** 100+ resorts in Switzerland and Austria, including Saas-Fee's full ski area
    - **Validity period:** typically runs from late October through to late April
    - **Youth & children:** discounted Magic Pass rates are available for younger skiers
    - **Limitations:** the Magic Pass may have restrictions on the number of days usable per resort at a given price tier — check the current terms before purchasing

    If you're a frequent recreational skier who visits more than two or three resorts per season, the Magic Pass will almost certainly pay for itself.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Included in the Pass

A standard Saastal ski pass gives you access to the full mountain — not just the lower lifts. Here's exactly what's covered:

- **All marked pistes** throughout the Saas-Fee ski area, including beginner, intermediate, and advanced runs
- **All lifts and gondolas** — the Alpin Express, Felskinn Cable Car, Morenia Gondola, Hannigalp Gondola, Langfluh Cable Car, and all surface lifts
- **Metro Alpin** — the world's highest underground funicular from Felskinn to Mittelallalin (3456m) is fully included; no supplement required
- **Free ski bus** between Saas-Fee, Saas-Grund, Saas-Almagell, and Saas-Balen — no need to drive or pay for transfers between the Saas Valley villages
- **Glacier area access** — the year-round glacier ski terrain at Mittelallalin is included in the standard winter pass

## Summer Saastal Card

The Saastal Card is the summer equivalent of the ski pass, designed for hikers, day-trippers, and village-based guests who want to make the most of the mountains without a car.

- **Coverage:** all mountain lifts in the Saastal — gondolas, cable cars, and hiking shuttle buses — giving you access to the full network of high-altitude hiking trails
- **Glacier lifts included:** the Alpin Express and Metro Alpin run through the summer season, and the Saastal Card gives you access to the glacier ski area lifts for summer skiing
- **Hotel guests:** the Saastal Card is included free with many hotel stays in the Saas Valley — check with your accommodation when booking, as this can represent significant added value
- **Day purchase:** if your hotel doesn't include the card, day and multi-day versions can be purchased at the ticket office or online
- **Activities:** the card also covers certain hiking buses and shuttle services that connect trailheads across the valley

Whether you're planning a dawn glacier hike to Mittelallalin, an afternoon stroll from Hannigalp, or a full traverse from Längfluh, the Saastal Card ensures you spend your day walking — not worrying about transport costs.

## Children & Youth

Saas-Fee actively encourages families on the slopes with generous discounts for younger skiers.

- **Children under 9:** ski free when accompanied by a paying adult (seasonal terms and conditions apply — confirm the current age cut-off when purchasing)
- **Youth passes (ages 9–19):** available at significantly reduced rates compared to adult passes; bring proof of age when collecting your pass
- **Family passes:** purpose-built family pass products offer further savings for two adults skiing with children — check the current family pricing on the Saas-Fee website
- **Ski school packages:** discounted ski school lessons are often bundled with youth passes; ask at the ticket office or your hotel for current offers

<Info>
  Pass prices change each season. Always check the current rates on the official Saas-Fee website before booking to ensure you're budgeting accurately.
</Info>

## Where to Buy

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Online" icon="globe">
    Purchase at **saas-fee.ch** before you travel. Online booking is available for all pass types and is the most convenient option. Your pass is loaded onto a card that you collect on arrival, or sent directly to your hotel in some cases.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ticket Office" icon="ticket">
    The main ticket office is located at the base of the **Alpin Express gondola** in the village centre. Open daily during the ski season from early morning. Be prepared for queues during peak periods and on powder days.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Hotel Reception" icon="building">
    Many hotels in Saas-Fee can arrange passes on your behalf — particularly useful if you're arriving late or want to avoid a detour to the ticket office on your first morning. Speak to reception when you check in.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Buying your pass online in advance typically saves 5–10% compared to purchasing at the ticket office window. It also means you can head straight to the lifts on your first morning rather than joining the queue.
</Tip>
